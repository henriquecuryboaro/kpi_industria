
================================================================================
CHEMICAL INDUSTRY KPI DATASET - DOCUMENTATION
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
This synthetic dataset contains operational and financial data for 5 chemical 
production facilities across 3 years (2023-2025), enabling comprehensive KPI 
analysis including EBITDA and other critical metrics.

Dataset Size: 5 facilities × ~36 months = 103 records

================================================================================
DATASET STRUCTURE
================================================================================

1. PRODUCTION FACILITIES (chemical_facilities.csv.gz)
   - facility_id: Unique facility identifier
   - facility_name: Name of the production facility
   - location: City and country
   - facility_type: Type of chemical production
   - capacity_tons_per_year: Annual production capacity
   - employees: Number of employees

2. MONTHLY OPERATIONS (chemical_monthly_operations.csv.gz)
   Raw operational and financial data by facility and month

3. COMPLETE KPI DATASET (chemical_industry_kpis_complete.csv.gz)
   All raw data plus calculated KPIs (52 columns total)

================================================================================
FINANCIAL KPIs
================================================================================

PRIMARY PROFITABILITY METRICS:

1. EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization)
   Formula: Gross Profit - Operating Expenses + Depreciation
   Or: Revenue - COGS - Operating Expenses + Depreciation

2. EBITDA Margin (%)
   Formula: (EBITDA / Revenue) × 100
   Benchmark: 10-20% is typical for chemical industry

3. EBIT (Earnings Before Interest and Taxes)
   Formula: EBITDA - Depreciation

4. EBIT Margin (%)
   Formula: (EBIT / Revenue) × 100

5. Net Income
   Formula: EBIT - Interest Expense - Tax Expense

6. Net Margin (%)
   Formula: (Net Income / Revenue) × 100

7. Gross Profit
   Formula: Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

8. Gross Margin (%)
   Formula: (Gross Profit / Revenue) × 100

COST STRUCTURE:
- Revenue: Total sales revenue (thousands USD)
- COGS: Cost of Goods Sold (60-75% of revenue)
  - Raw Material Costs (largest component)
  - Labor Costs
  - Energy Costs
  - Maintenance Costs
  - Depreciation
- Operating Expenses: SG&A costs (10-20% of revenue)
- Interest Expense: Cost of debt
- Tax Expense: Corporate taxes

================================================================================
OPERATIONAL KPIs
================================================================================

PRODUCTION EFFICIENCY:

1. Capacity Utilization (%)
   Formula: (Actual Production / Maximum Capacity) × 100
   Benchmark: 80-95% is optimal

2. Yield Rate (%)
   Formula: (Actual Output / Theoretical Output) × 100
   Benchmark: 85-98% for chemical processes

3. Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE %)
   Formula: Availability × Performance × Quality
   - Availability = (Total Time - Downtime) / Total Time
   - Performance = Capacity Utilization
   - Quality = 100% - Defect Rate
   Benchmark: >85% is world-class

4. Production Efficiency Score
   Weighted composite: 40% Capacity + 30% Yield + 30% Batch Success

5. Downtime Hours
   Unplanned production stoppages per month

QUALITY METRICS:

6. Quality Defect Rate (%)
   Percentage of production with quality issues
   Benchmark: <2% is good

7. Batch Success Rate (%)
   Percentage of batches completed successfully
   Benchmark: >95% is good

COST EFFICIENCY:

8. Cost per Ton Produced (USD)
   Formula: (COGS × 1000) / Production Volume

9. Revenue per Ton (USD)
   Formula: (Revenue × 1000) / Production Volume

10. Profit per Ton (USD)
    Formula: Revenue per Ton - Cost per Ton

RESOURCE EFFICIENCY:

11. Energy per Ton (MWh/ton)
    Formula: Energy Consumption / Production Volume

12. Water per Ton (m³/ton)
    Formula: Water Usage / Production Volume

13. Waste per Ton Produced
    Formula: Waste Generated / Production Volume

LABOR PRODUCTIVITY:

14. Revenue per Employee (USD)
    Formula: (Revenue × 1000) / Number of Employees

15. Production per Employee (tons)
    Formula: Production Volume / Number of Employees

SAFETY & ENVIRONMENTAL:

16. Safety Score (0-100)
    Formula: 100 - (Safety Incidents × 20)
    Benchmark: >95 is excellent

17. Environmental Score (0-100)
    Formula: 100 - (Environmental Violations × 30)
    Benchmark: 100 is target

18. Safety Incidents
    Number of safety incidents per month

19. Environmental Violations
    Number of environmental violations per month

SAMPLE QUERIES & ANALYSIS
================================================================================

1. Calculate EBITDA by Facility:
   GROUP BY facility_name, SUM(ebitda), AVG(ebitda_margin_pct)

2. Identify Best Performing Facilities:
   ORDER BY oee_pct DESC, ebitda_margin_pct DESC

3. Trend Analysis:
   GROUP BY year_month to see performance over time

4. Cost Optimization Opportunities:
   Identify facilities with high cost_per_ton or low profit_per_ton

5. Efficiency Benchmarking:
   Compare capacity_utilization_pct, oee_pct across facilities
